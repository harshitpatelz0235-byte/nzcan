🏷️ Product Descriptions & Listings

1

High-Converting Product Description (Shopify/Website)

You are an expert D2C copywriter for the Indian e-commerce market. Write a high-converting product description for: Product Name: [e.g., "GlowUp Vitamin C Serum"] Product Category: [e.g., skincare / fashion / electronics / food] Price: ₹[Price] Key Ingredients/Materials: [e.g., 20% Vitamin C, Hyaluronic Acid, natural extracts] Key Benefits (list 3-5): [e.g., reduces dark spots, hydrates skin, visible results in 2 weeks] Target Customer: [e.g., women 22-35 in metro cities, skin-conscious] Brand Voice: [e.g., fun & friendly / clinical & trustworthy / luxurious] Write the description in this exact structure: 1. HEADLINE: A desire-triggering headline (under 10 words) 2. HOOK: Opening 2 sentences that address the customer's pain point 3. BENEFITS: 4-5 bullet points — each starting with an emoji, benefit-first (not feature-first) 4. SOCIAL PROOF LINE: A line mentioning popularity (e.g., "Trusted by 10,000+ women across India") 5. INGREDIENTS/DETAILS: Brief, scannable section 6. CTA: A compelling call-to-action with urgency Rules: - Write in conversational Indian English - Use sensory language (how it feels, looks, smells) - Include 1 Hindi/Hinglish phrase naturally - Keep total length under 250 words - Make it scannable on mobile (short paragraphs)
💡 Pro Tip: After generating, read only the headline + first 2 lines. If they don't make you want to keep reading, regenerate with "make the opening more emotionally provocative."

OPTIMIZED TITLE: Follow Amazon format — Brand + Key Feature + Product + Size/Variant + Target (max 200 characters) 2. 5 BULLET POINTS: Each under 200 characters, starting with a CAPITALIZED benefit phrase, include keywords naturally 3. A+ CONTENT DESCRIPTION: 300-word rich description with headers, lifestyle scenarios, and comparison to alternatives 4. BACKEND SEARCH TERMS: 20 comma-separated keywords (include Hindi transliterations like "chamak", "sundar", "safed") 5. SEARCH TERMS TO TARGET: Top 10 keywords a customer would type to find this product Rules: - Include both English and Hindi search terms - Reference Indian festivals, seasons, and occasions where relevant - Include "gift for" keywords (Indians search heavily for gifts)
💡 Pro Tip: Use the backend search terms in your Flipkart listing too. Flipkart's search algorithm also benefits from Hindi transliteration keywords that most sellers miss.

Product Comparison Page Writer

Create a compelling "Why Choose Us" comparison page for my D2C website: My Product: [Product Name & key details] My Price: ₹[Price] Competitor 1: [Name, price, weakness] Competitor 2: [Name, price, weakness] My Unique Advantages: [list 3-4 genuine advantages] Write: 1. A headline that positions my brand as the obvious winner 2. A comparison table (feature | My Brand | Competitor 1 | Competitor 2) with ✓ and ✗ marks 3. 3 short "why we're different" paragraphs 4. A trust-building section (made in India, certifications, customer count) 5. A closing CTA paragraph Rules: - Be factual, not slanderous — highlight our strengths, not their failures - Emphasize value-for-money (critical for Indian buyers) - Include "Made in India" / "Proudly Indian" if applicable - Write in confident but not arrogant tone
💡 Pro Tip: Indian customers are extremely value-conscious. The comparison table is the most-read part — make sure your "per unit cost" or "cost per use" math makes you look better.

Bundle/Combo Product Naming & Description

I'm creating product bundles for my D2C store. Help me name and describe them: Products in Bundle: [list all items included] Individual Prices: [price of each item] Bundle Price: ₹[discounted bundle price] Target Occasion: [e.g., Diwali gifting / wedding season / daily use combo / starter kit] Target Customer: [e.g., new mothers / college students / corporate gifting] Generate: 1. 5 catchy bundle names (mix of English and Hindi/Hinglish options) 2. A 100-word bundle description highlighting the savings and convenience 3. "What's Inside" section with each item + its individual value 4. A "total value vs. bundle price" savings callout 5. 3 urgency-driven CTA variations Make it feel like a curated gift, not a random dump of products.
💡 Pro Tip: Bundles named after festivals (e.g., "The Diwali Glow Kit") sell 2-3x better than generic names. Time your bundles around Indian festivals for maximum impact.

Customer Review Response Generator

You are a customer success manager for an Indian D2C brand. Write thoughtful responses to these customer reviews: Brand Name: [Your Brand] Brand Voice: [warm & caring / professional / quirky] Review 1 (Positive): [paste the positive review] Review 2 (Negative): [paste the negative review] Review 3 (Neutral/Question): [paste the neutral review] For each review, write a response that: - Addresses the customer by name (if visible) - Acknowledges their specific feedback (not generic "thank you") - For positive: encourages them to try another product or share with friends - For negative: empathizes, offers a solution, provides WhatsApp/email for direct resolution - For neutral: answers the question and adds useful information - Ends with a warm sign-off Keep each response under 80 words. Sound human, not corporate.
💡 Pro Tip: Respond to every negative review within 4 hours. Indian customers heavily read negative reviews before buying — your response is your second chance to convert their audience.

📣 Facebook & Instagram Ad Copy

6

Facebook Ad Copy Generator (3 Variations)

You are an expert D2C copywriter specializing in Meta Ads for the Indian market. Write 3 variations of a Facebook/Instagram ad for: Product: [Product Name] Price: ₹[Price] Target Audience: [e.g., women aged 25-35 in metro cities] Key Benefit: [e.g., organic ingredients, fast delivery] Offer: [e.g., 30% off, free shipping, buy 1 get 1] Tone: [e.g., casual & friendly / premium / urgent] For EACH variation, write: 1. A scroll-stopping headline (under 8 words — this appears below the image) 2. Primary text (under 125 words) — use a different angle for each: - Variation 1: Problem-Solution angle - Variation 2: Social Proof angle ("Join 5,000+ women who...") - Variation 3: Urgency/Scarcity angle ("Only 48 hours left...") 3. A clear CTA line (e.g., "Shop now → Link in bio") Rules: - Include at least 1 Hindi/Hinglish phrase naturally per variation - Use price anchoring (compare to expensive alternatives) - Reference Indian context (festivals, weather, city life) - Add 2-3 emojis per variation (not overdone) - Write for mobile-first (short lines, easy to scan while scrolling)
💡 Pro Tip: Run this prompt 3 times and pick the best headline from each batch. Test the 3 best headlines against each other in your Facebook ad set — headline is responsible for 80% of your CTR.
7

Instagram Reel Ad Script (15-30 Seconds)

Write a 15-30 second Instagram Reel ad script for: Product: [Product Name] Key Benefit: [main benefit in 5 words] Target Viewer: [who is scrolling past this] Offer: [current offer/discount] Script format: HOOK (0-3 sec): The first thing said/shown that stops the scroll. Must be shocking, relatable, or curiosity-driven. PROBLEM (3-8 sec): Quickly establish the pain point the viewer relates to. SOLUTION (8-18 sec): Introduce the product as the answer. Show it in action. PROOF (18-25 sec): Quick social proof — customer count, rating, testimonial quote. CTA (25-30 sec): Tell them exactly what to do. Create urgency. Also include: - Text overlay suggestions for each segment - Background music mood recommendation - 3 caption options (one with a question to drive comments) - 10 hashtags (mix of niche + broad Indian hashtags)
💡 Pro Tip: The hook (first 3 seconds) decides everything. If your video's average watch time is under 3 seconds, the hook failed. Test hooks like "POV: You finally found..." or "Stop buying [category] until you see this."
8

Retargeting Ad Copy (for Cart Abandoners)

Write 3 retargeting ad variations for people who visited my product page or added to cart but didn't buy: Product: [Product Name] Price: ₹[Price] Special Retarget Offer: [e.g., extra 10% off, free gift, extended warranty] For each variation, use a different psychological trigger: - Variation 1: FOMO ("You left something behind... and it's almost sold out") - Variation 2: Testimonial Push ("Here's what [Name] said after buying...") - Variation 3: Direct Offer ("We saved your cart + added a surprise") Each ad needs: 1. Headline (under 8 words) 2. Primary text (under 80 words) 3. CTA text Sound personal, like a friend reminding them — not pushy.
💡 Pro Tip: Retargeting ads should run 1-7 days after cart abandonment. After 7 days, move them to a "brand awareness" audience. Indian buyers often need 3-4 touchpoints before purchasing.

Festival Sale Campaign Copy (Complete)

Create a complete festival sale campaign for my D2C brand: Brand: [Brand Name] Festival: [e.g., Diwali / Holi / Raksha Bandhan / Christmas / New Year] Offer: [e.g., flat 40% off / buy 2 get 1 / free gift with purchase] Sale Duration: [e.g., Oct 20-31] Hero Products: [list 2-3 products to push] Generate a complete campaign with: 1. CAMPAIGN NAME: 3 catchy names using festival + brand wordplay 2. TEASER AD (3 days before): "Something big is coming" copy 3. LAUNCH AD (Day 1): Full announcement with offer details 4. REMINDER AD (Mid-sale): "Don't miss out" copy with time pressure 5. LAST CHANCE AD (Final day): Maximum urgency copy 6. INSTAGRAM STORIES: 5 story slide scripts (tap-through sequence) 7. WHATSAPP BROADCAST: Sale announcement message 8. EMAIL SUBJECT LINES: 5 options with open-rate optimization Make everything feel festive, warm, and celebratory — not just "discount discount discount."
💡 Pro Tip: Start teaser ads 5-7 days before the festival for maximum anticipation. Diwali and New Year are the highest-ROAS periods for D2C brands in India — plan your best offers for these.

UGC (User-Generated Content) Ad Script Brief

Write a UGC creator brief for an authentic-feeling Instagram ad: Product: [Product Name] Creator Type: [e.g., young woman in her 20s, mom, college student] Video Style: [e.g., "get ready with me", unboxing, day-in-my-life, talking head] Key Message: [what the viewer should take away] Write: 1. BRIEF FOR CREATOR: Step-by-step shooting instructions 2. TALKING POINTS: What to say naturally (NOT a rigid script — talking points they can adapt) 3. B-ROLL SHOTS: 5 specific shots to capture (close-up of product, using it, reaction, etc.) 4. HOOK OPTIONS: 3 opening lines the creator can choose from 5. CTA: How they should end the video The final video should feel like a friend recommending something on their stories — NOT a scripted ad.
💡 Pro Tip: UGC ads outperform polished brand ads by 4x on Meta in India. Find micro-influencers (5K-50K followers) on Instagram who already post in your niche. They charge ₹2,000-₹10,000 per video.

📱 WhatsApp Marketing Messages

11

WhatsApp Broadcast — New Product Launch

Write a WhatsApp broadcast message for a new product launch: Product: [Product Name] Price: ₹[Price] Key Benefit: [main selling point] Launch Offer: [e.g., early bird 20% off for first 100 customers] Order Link: [your website/Instamojo link] Write 2 versions: Version 1: Short & punchy (under 60 words) — for blast to full list Version 2: Detailed & personal (under 120 words) — for VIP/repeat customers Rules: - Start with a personalized greeting ("Hey! 👋") - Use emojis naturally (not excessive) - Include a clear CTA with the link - Add urgency ("only 100 units" or "offer ends tonight") - Feel like a message from a friend, not a company - Include Hindi/Hinglish if the brand voice allows
💡 Pro Tip: Send WhatsApp broadcasts between 10-11 AM or 7-8 PM IST for highest open rates. Avoid sending on Mondays (people are overwhelmed) and Sundays (low purchase intent).
12

WhatsApp Cart Recovery Sequence (3 Messages)

Create a 3-message WhatsApp sequence for customers who abandoned their cart: Brand: [Brand Name] Brand Voice: [friendly / premium / quirky] Write 3 messages: MESSAGE 1 (Send after 1 hour): - Casual reminder that they left items in cart - Include a "Need help deciding?" angle - Tone: helpful, not pushy MESSAGE 2 (Send after 24 hours): - Social proof angle (what others say about the product) - Address common objections (price, quality, shipping time) - Offer to answer questions via chat MESSAGE 3 (Send after 48 hours): - Final offer — exclusive small discount or free shipping - Create urgency — "cart expires in 24 hours" - Direct one-click checkout link Each message: under 80 words. Must feel human, NOT automated.
💡 Pro Tip: This 3-message sequence typically recovers 15-25% of abandoned carts. The secret is Message 2's social proof — include a real screenshot of a 5-star review in the WhatsApp message alongside the text.
13

Post-Purchase WhatsApp Sequence

Create a post-purchase WhatsApp message sequence to build loyalty and drive repeat purchases: Brand: [Brand Name] Product: [what they just bought] Estimated Delivery: [e.g., 3-5 days] Reorder Cycle: [how often they'd need to buy again — e.g., monthly for skincare] Write 5 messages: MSG 1 — Order Confirmation (immediately): Thank them, confirm order, share tracking info MSG 2 — Delivery Day: "Your package is arriving today!" + excitement builder MSG 3 — Day 3 after delivery: Check-in + usage tips + request for feedback MSG 4 — Day 14: Ask for a Google/Amazon review (with direct review link) MSG 5 — Day 25 (before reorder time): Gentle nudge to reorder + exclusive repeat-buyer discount Each under 60 words. Warm, personal, never salesy.
💡 Pro Tip: Message 4 (review request) is pure gold. Indian consumers trust reviews more than ads. One genuine review = 10 potential customers. Include a direct Google review link to make it one-tap easy.

Welcome Email Sequence (5 Emails)

Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers/first-time visitors of my D2C brand: Brand: [Brand Name] What We Sell: [product category + key differentiator] Brand Story (brief): [founder story, why you started] Welcome Offer: [e.g., 15% off first order with code WELCOME15] Bestseller Products: [list top 3 products] Write 5 emails with subject lines: EMAIL 1 (Day 0): Welcome + deliver the promised discount code + brief brand intro EMAIL 2 (Day 2): Brand story — why we started, what makes us different EMAIL 3 (Day 4): Social proof — customer testimonials + bestsellers showcase EMAIL 4 (Day 6): Education — how to use/choose the right product + tips EMAIL 5 (Day 8): Last-chance reminder for welcome discount (expires tomorrow) For each email provide: - Subject line (under 50 characters) + preview text - Full email body (under 150 words each) - CTA button text Make emails conversational, not corporate. Think "founder writing to a friend."
💡 Pro Tip: Email 1 open rates in India average 45-60%. By Email 5, it drops to 15-20%. Front-load your most important message (the offer) in Email 1, and make Email 5's subject line urgent: "⏰ Your 15% off expires in 24 hours."

Win-Back Email for Inactive Customers

Write a win-back email for customers who haven't purchased in 60+ days: Brand: [Brand Name] Customer Segment: [e.g., bought once 3 months ago / was a regular, stopped buying] Win-Back Offer: [e.g., flat ₹200 off / free shipping / free sample with order] New Products/Updates: [anything new since their last purchase] Write: - 3 subject line options (one emotional, one curiosity-driven, one offer-first) - Email body (under 120 words) that: 1. Acknowledges they've been away (without being guilt-trippy) 2. Shares what's new 3. Offers a personal-feeling incentive 4. Has a single clear CTA - PS line that adds a human touch
💡 Pro Tip: Subject line "We miss you (and we brought gifts)" has a 35% higher open rate than "Come back and shop." Emotional subject lines outperform discount-first lines for win-back emails in India.

🔗 Advanced Workflow Chains

C1

Complete Product Launch Chain (4 Steps)

This is a sequential workflow. Run each step in order, feeding the output of each step into the next. STEP 1 — Market Research Prompt: "I'm launching [product] in the Indian market. Analyze the top 5 competitors in this space. For each, list: their positioning, price point, best-selling variant, and 2 weaknesses I can exploit. Also identify 3 underserved customer segments in India for this product category." STEP 2 — Positioning Statement (feed Step 1 output): "Based on this competitive analysis, write a brand positioning statement for my product using the format: For [target customer] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit] unlike [competitors] because [reason to believe]." STEP 3 — Launch Copy Suite (feed Step 2 output): "Using this positioning, create: 1 product description (200 words), 3 Facebook ad headline variations, 1 Instagram launch caption, and 1 WhatsApp broadcast message. Keep all copy consistent with the positioning." STEP 4 — Launch Week Calendar (feed Step 3 output): "Create a day-by-day launch week content calendar (Day -3 teaser to Day +4 post-launch). For each day: what to post on Instagram, what to send on WhatsApp, and what email to send. Include the copy from Step 3 where relevant."
💡 Pro Tip: This 4-step chain replaces what a marketing agency would charge ₹50,000+ for a product launch plan. Run it once, customize the outputs, and you have a complete go-to-market strategy.
C2

Customer Avatar Deep Dive Chain (3 Steps)

STEP 1 — Avatar Builder: "I sell [product] priced at ₹[price] in India. Build a detailed customer avatar including: demographics (age, gender, city tier, income), psychographics (values, fears, aspirations), daily routine, media consumption (which Instagram pages they follow, which YouTube channels, which apps), purchase triggers, and common objections before buying." STEP 2 — Pain Point Map (feed Step 1): "Based on this avatar, list their top 10 pain points related to [product category]. For each pain point, rate the intensity (low/medium/high) and identify which of my product's features directly solves it. Also note which pain points competitors are NOT addressing." STEP 3 — Message Matrix (feed Step 2): "Using the avatar and pain point map, create a messaging matrix with 5 ad angles. For each angle: the core emotional trigger, a sample headline, a sample opening line, and which platform it works best on (Facebook feed / Instagram Reels / WhatsApp / Email)."
💡 Pro Tip: Run this chain for EACH product you sell. Save the avatar document and reference it every time you write new marketing copy. Consistent targeting = lower cost per acquisition.

⚡ Quick-Fire Prompts (Copy & Go)

Q1

5 CTA Variations

"Write 5 different call-to-action button texts for my product page selling [product] at ₹[price]. Make each one unique: 1 urgency-based, 1 benefit-based, 1 curiosity-based, 1 social-proof-based, 1 value-based. Keep each under 5 words."
Q2

Hashtag Research

"Generate 30 Instagram hashtags for an Indian D2C brand selling [product category]. Group them into: 10 broad hashtags (100K+ posts), 10 niche hashtags (10K-100K posts), and 10 micro hashtags (under 10K posts). Include India-specific hashtags and Hindi hashtags."
Q3

Objection Handler Script

"My most common customer objections for [product] are: [list 3-5 objections, e.g., 'too expensive', 'will it work for my skin type?', 'I can get cheaper on Amazon']. For each objection, write a 2-3 sentence WhatsApp reply that addresses the concern with empathy, evidence, and a redirect to purchase."
Q4

Unboxing Experience Script

"I want to create a memorable unboxing experience for my D2C brand [Brand Name]. Write: 1) A handwritten-style thank you note (under 40 words), 2) A product usage tip card (3 tips), 3) A fun insert card encouraging them to share an unboxing video on Instagram with our hashtag #[BrandHashtag] for a chance to win a free product."
Q5

Google My Business Description

"Write a Google My Business description for my D2C brand [Brand Name] that sells [products]. Location: [City, India]. Include relevant local keywords, mention delivery options (all-India shipping), and highlight what makes us unique. Keep under 750 characters."
