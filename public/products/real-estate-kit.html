🏗️ Property Listing Descriptions

1

Premium Property Listing Writer (99acres / MagicBricks / Housing.com)

You are a premium real estate copywriter for the Indian property market. Write a compelling listing description for: Property Type: [e.g., 3BHK Flat / 4BHK Villa / Commercial Office Space / 1BHK Studio] Project/Builder Name: [e.g., Prestige Lakeside Habitat / independent construction] Location: [e.g., Whitefield, Bangalore / Baner, Pune / Noida Sector 150] Area: [e.g., 1,450 sq ft carpet / 2,200 sq ft super built-up] Price: ₹[e.g., 85 Lakhs / 1.2 Crore] Floor: [e.g., 12th floor of 22 floors / ground floor with garden] Furnishing: [fully furnished / semi-furnished / unfurnished] Key Features: [e.g., modular kitchen, 2 balconies, gated community, gym, swimming pool, parking] Nearby Landmarks: [e.g., 5 min from Phoenix Mall, 2 km from metro, near international school] Target Buyer: [e.g., IT professionals / NRIs / families with kids / investors] Transaction Type: [sale / rent / lease] Write: 1. HEADLINE: A desire-triggering headline (NOT "3BHK for sale in Whitefield") - Use formats like "Wake Up to Lake Views..." or "Your Dream Home in the Heart of..." 2. DESCRIPTION (150-200 words): Paint a lifestyle picture — describe the LIFE, not just the flat 3. KEY HIGHLIGHTS: 8-10 bullet points with emojis 4. LOCALITY ADVANTAGES: 5 points about the area (connectivity, schools, hospitals, malls) 5. IDEAL FOR: Who this property is perfect for + why 6. CTA: Contact prompt with urgency ("Only 2 units left on this floor") Optimize for search: Include locality name, property type, and budget range naturally. Use Indian English (flat, not apartment; lakh/crore, not million).
💡 Pro Tip: Properties with detailed, emotional descriptions get 3x more inquiries on 99acres than basic listings. Copy-paste this output directly into the Description field. Add 10+ photos for maximum visibility.
2

Rental Property Listing Optimizer

Optimize this rental property listing for maximum tenant inquiries: Property: [Type, location, size] Monthly Rent: ₹[rent] Security Deposit: ₹[deposit] Available From: [date] Preferred Tenant: [family / bachelors / working professionals / anyone] Furnishing Details: [list items included — AC, washing machine, beds, etc.] Society Amenities: [gym, pool, park, security, power backup] Distance to Key Places: [office hubs, metro, market, hospital] Write: 1. HEADLINE that appeals to the target tenant persona 2. DESCRIPTION focusing on convenience and daily life quality 3. "WHAT'S INCLUDED" detailed list (so tenants know exactly what they get) 4. HOUSE RULES stated politely (if any — pets, smoking, etc.) 5. CTA with availability urgency Tone: Professional yet welcoming. Make the tenant feel like this is already their home.
💡 Pro Tip: Mention "power backup" and "water supply" in every Noida/Gurgaon listing — these are top concerns for renters. For Bangalore, mention "traffic-free commute" and "metro connectivity."
3

Neighborhood / Locality Deep-Dive Description

Write a comprehensive neighborhood guide for use in property listings and client conversations: Locality: [e.g., HSR Layout, Bangalore / Bandra West, Mumbai / DLF Phase 5, Gurgaon] Property Price Range in Area: ₹[range, e.g., 60L - 1.5Cr] Type of Buyers Here: [IT professionals / families / luxury buyers / students] Research and write about: 1. LIFESTYLE OVERVIEW: What it feels like to live here (200 words) 2. CONNECTIVITY: Metro/bus, highways, airport distance, traffic patterns 3. EDUCATION: Top 5 schools and colleges nearby 4. HEALTHCARE: Major hospitals within 5 km 5. SHOPPING & DINING: Malls, markets, popular restaurants 6. IT/OFFICE HUBS: Major tech parks and corporate offices nearby 7. SAFETY & INFRASTRUCTURE: CCTV, police station, water/electricity reliability 8. FUTURE DEVELOPMENT: Upcoming metro lines, road projects, new commercial zones 9. INVESTMENT OUTLOOK: Price appreciation trend, rental yield estimate 10. BEST FOR: Which type of buyer/tenant this area is ideal for Include specific names of landmarks, not generic descriptions.
💡 Pro Tip: Save locality descriptions for your top 10 areas. Re-use them in every listing, client presentation, and social media post about that area. This builds you as the "go-to expert" for that locality.
4

NRI Buyer Property Presentation

Create a professional property presentation email for an NRI buyer: Property Details: [type, location, area, price, key features] NRI's Current Location: [e.g., USA / UAE / UK / Singapore] Their Purpose: [investment / retirement home / family relocation / rental income] Their Budget: ₹[budget range] Concerns They've Mentioned: [e.g., legal paperwork from abroad, rental management, RERA compliance] Write a detailed email that includes: 1. Personalized greeting acknowledging their situation (living abroad, investing in India) 2. Property walkthrough description (they can't visit, so make it vivid) 3. Investment rationale (expected appreciation, rental yield in %) 4. Legal assurance section (RERA registration, clear title, bank-approved) 5. "How We Help NRIs" section — address their specific concerns 6. Virtual tour / video call offer 7. Next steps clearly laid out Tone: Professional, trustworthy, patient (NRIs need more hand-holding than local buyers).
💡 Pro Tip: NRI buyers spend 2-3x more time researching before deciding. Include RERA registration numbers and bank-approval status in every communication — this is their #1 trust signal.

💬 Client Follow-Up Messages

5

WhatsApp Follow-Up After Site Visit (3-Message Sequence)

Write a 3-message WhatsApp follow-up sequence for a client who visited a property: Property They Visited: [name, location, type] Client Name: [Name] Visit Date: [when they visited] Their Reaction During Visit: [positive / interested but hesitant / comparing options] Their Main Concern: [e.g., price too high / location concern / wants more options / needs family approval] MESSAGE 1 (Same evening after visit): - Thank them for visiting - Recall something specific they liked - Ask if they have questions - Tone: warm, no pressure MESSAGE 2 (Next day): - Address their main concern proactively - Share a relevant fact (market data, similar sale, upcoming development) - Offer to send documents or arrange a second visit - Tone: helpful expert MESSAGE 3 (Day 3): - Create gentle urgency (other interested parties, price revision upcoming) - Offer a specific next step (meeting with builder, documentation discussion) - Include a "take your time, but here's why acting soon helps" angle - Tone: professional friend Each message: under 60 words. Human, not template-y.
💡 Pro Tip: Message 1 sent within 4 hours of the visit has a 3x higher response rate than next-day follow-ups. Indian buyers appreciate promptness — it signals seriousness.
6

Cold Lead Reactivation Messages

Write messages to re-engage leads who went cold (inquired 30-90 days ago but stopped responding): Your Name: [Your Name] Lead's Name: [Lead Name] What They Were Looking For: [e.g., 2BHK in Pune under ₹60L] Last Interaction: [what was the last conversation about] Write 3 different re-engagement messages (use different approaches): 1. VALUE-FIRST: Share a market insight or new listing that matches their criteria 2. CHECK-IN: Casual, human check-in ("Still looking? Things have changed in the market...") 3. SCARCITY: Inform about a price change or new project launch relevant to them Rules: - Never say "I noticed you haven't responded" (guilt-inducing) - Lead with new information, not old conversation - Keep each under 50 words - Include a low-friction CTA (just reply yes/no, not "schedule a call")
💡 Pro Tip: The "value-first" message works best. Send a screenshot of a new listing that matches their old criteria with "Saw this and immediately thought of you. Is this closer to what you were looking for?"
7

Price Negotiation Response Scripts

Help me respond to common price negotiation scenarios from buyers: Property Price: ₹[asking price] My Minimum Acceptable: ₹[lowest I can go] Property USP: [why this property is worth the price] Write professional responses for these scenarios: SCENARIO 1: "Your price is too high. I can get similar flats for ₹[lower amount] nearby." SCENARIO 2: "I'll buy immediately if you reduce by [X] lakhs." SCENARIO 3: "My budget is only ₹[their budget]. Can you adjust?" SCENARIO 4: "I need to discuss with my family. Will decide in 2 weeks." SCENARIO 5: "I'm comparing with 3 other properties." For each scenario: - Acknowledge their position empathetically - Justify the value (not just defend the price) - Offer a creative solution (payment flexibility, added value, smaller concession) - Keep the conversation moving forward - Never sound desperate or annoyed
💡 Pro Tip: The #1 rule in Indian real estate negotiation: never drop price without getting something in return ("I can do ₹5L less if we close documentation this week"). This maintains your authority.
8

Client Testimonial Request Message

Write a WhatsApp message asking a satisfied client for a testimonial: Client Name: [Name] Property They Bought/Rented: [details] When the Deal Closed: [date/timeframe] Something Special You Did for Them: [e.g., found the perfect flat in 1 week, negotiated a great price] Write: 1. A warm opening acknowledging the milestone (1 month since they moved in, etc.) 2. A genuine ask for feedback (not "write me a review" — more natural) 3. 3 guided questions they can answer (to make it easy for them): - What was your biggest concern before buying, and how did we address it? - What surprised you most about the experience? - Would you recommend us? If yes, why? 4. Mention where you'll use it (Google reviews / Instagram / website) 5. Ask if they'd be open to a short video testimonial (phone selfie is fine) Tone: Grateful, humble, specific — NOT "kindly provide a review for my business."
💡 Pro Tip: The best time to ask for a testimonial: 2-4 weeks after they move in. They're still in the "honeymoon phase" with their new home. Send the message on a weekend evening when they're relaxed.

📱 Social Media Content

9

Instagram Reel Script — Property Tour Video

Write a 30-60 second Instagram Reel script for a property tour video: Property: [type, location, area, price] Key Selling Points: [3-4 highlights] Target Viewer: [who is this Reel for — first-time buyers? investors? renters?] Write a shot-by-shot script: SHOT 1 (0-3 sec): HOOK — What to say/show to stop the scroll Examples: "₹75 lakhs mei aisa flat milega? 😱" or "This view at this price won't last" SHOT 2 (3-8 sec): EXTERIOR — What to say while showing the building/entrance SHOT 3 (8-15 sec): LIVING ROOM — Highlight space, natural light, view SHOT 4 (15-22 sec): KITCHEN — Focus on storage, modular fittings SHOT 5 (22-28 sec): BEDROOM + BALCONY — Show the best angle SHOT 6 (28-35 sec): AMENITIES — Quick cuts of gym, pool, garden SHOT 7 (35-40 sec): REVEAL — Price, size, and "only X units left" SHOT 8 (40-45 sec): CTA — "DM me for a site visit" or "Link in bio" Also provide: - Text overlay suggestions for each shot - Music/audio mood recommendation - Caption with hashtags - 3 comment-engagement question options
💡 Pro Tip: Property tour Reels with a Hindi/Hinglish hook get 2-3x more engagement than English-only hooks in India. Start with something relatable: "Agar budget tight hai, but taste nahi..." — instant connection.
10

30-Day Real Estate Instagram Content Calendar

Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for a real estate agent/agency: Your Name/Agency: [Name] City/Area Focus: [where you operate] Specialization: [residential / commercial / luxury / affordable / rental] Target Audience: [first-time buyers / investors / NRIs / renters] Content Mix: - 30% Property Showcases (listings, tours, new launches) - 25% Educational (buying tips, legal advice, market insights) - 20% Personal Brand (your story, behind-the-scenes, client meetings) - 15% Client Stories (testimonials, handover ceremonies, before/after) - 10% Engagement (polls, myths vs facts, "which flat would you choose?") For each day provide: - Content type: Reel / Carousel / Story / Post - Topic + Hook - 3-bullet content outline - Purpose tag (Showcase / Educate / Trust / Engage) Include: - 2 "myth-busting" posts (common buyer misconceptions) - 2 market update posts - 1 "day in the life of a real estate agent" Reel - 1 trending audio/meme adaptation
💡 Pro Tip: "Which flat would you choose? A or B" comparison posts get massive engagement and DMs. Use this format weekly — it generates leads organically without feeling like an ad.
11

LinkedIn Post for Agent Personal Branding

Write a LinkedIn post to build my personal brand as a real estate expert: My Name: [Name] My City: [City] Topic: [Choose: market insight / deal story / industry prediction / client success / myth-busting] Key Point: [the main message you want to convey] Any Data/Numbers: [e.g., prices went up 12% in this area, helped 50 families this year] Write a LinkedIn post that: 1. Opens with a bold/contrarian first line (this appears before "...see more") 2. Tells a brief story or shares a specific insight 3. Includes 2-3 data points 4. Provides actionable advice for the reader 5. Ends with an engagement question 6. Includes relevant hashtags (5-8) Length: 150-200 words. Tone: authoritative but approachable. Format: Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences each) with line breaks for readability.
💡 Pro Tip: Post on LinkedIn Tuesday-Thursday, 8-9 AM IST, for maximum reach. Real estate agents who post 3x/week on LinkedIn report 40% of their high-value leads coming from the platform.
12

Market Update Newsletter / Email

Write a monthly market update email/newsletter for my client database: City/Area: [your market area] Month: [e.g., November 2026] Key Market Trends: [list 2-3 trends — prices up/down, new launches, policy changes] Your New Listings: [list 2-3 featured properties] Your Recent Deals: [any notable closings] Industry News: [RERA updates, interest rate changes, govt schemes] Write: 1. SUBJECT LINE: 3 options (one data-driven, one curiosity-driven, one news-driven) 2. OPENING: Brief, personal market commentary (like a friend updating you) 3. MARKET SNAPSHOT: 3-4 key data points presented visually (use numbers prominently) 4. FEATURED PROPERTIES: 2-3 listings with mini-descriptions + links 5. EXPERT TIP: One actionable piece of advice for buyers/investors 6. CLOSING: Personal sign-off + CTA to schedule a consultation Total: under 400 words. Scannable, professional, data-rich.
💡 Pro Tip: Send this on the 1st of every month. Consistency builds authority. After 3 months, clients will start forwarding your newsletter to friends — this is your most powerful passive lead-gen tool.

🤝 Sales Scripts & Closing

13

First Call Script for New Leads

Write a first phone call script for a new property inquiry: Lead Source: [99acres / Facebook ad / referral / walk-in] Property They Inquired About: [details] Lead Type: [buyer / renter / investor] Write a natural-sounding call script with: 1. OPENING (15 sec): Introduce yourself, reference their inquiry specifically 2. QUALIFYING QUESTIONS (2 min): - Budget range confirmation - Timeline to move/invest - Must-have requirements (BHK, location, amenities) - Deal-breakers - Who else is involved in the decision (spouse, parents) 3. VALUE PROPOSITION (1 min): What you offer that other agents don't 4. NEXT STEP (30 sec): Schedule a site visit or send curated options 5. CLOSING: Confirm WhatsApp contact for sharing details Rules: - Listen more than you talk (script includes pause points) - Never hard-sell on the first call - Gather information, don't push properties - Sound knowledgeable, not desperate
💡 Pro Tip: The qualifying questions are the most important part. Understanding the decision-maker dynamics ("My parents will also be visiting the property") helps you tailor the site visit presentation.
14

Property Comparison Report for Clients

Create a professional property comparison report to help a client decide between options: Client Name: [Name] Their Requirements: [what they're looking for] Their Budget: ₹[budget] Property A: [name, location, area, price, key features, pros, cons] Property B: [name, location, area, price, key features, pros, cons] Property C: [name, location, area, price, key features, pros, cons] Generate a structured comparison report with: 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: 3-line overview of which property suits which need 2. COMPARISON TABLE: Location | Area | Price | Price/sq ft | Furnishing | Amenities | Connectivity | RERA Status 3. DETAILED ANALYSIS: For each property — lifestyle fit, investment potential, trade-offs 4. MY RECOMMENDATION: Which property I'd recommend and why (be specific) 5. NEXT STEPS: What the client should do to proceed Format it cleanly — this is a document they'll share with family for decision-making.
💡 Pro Tip: This comparison report positions you as a consultant, not just an agent. Clients share this with family — your name and expertise travel with it. Include your branding and contact at the bottom.
15

Home Loan & Documentation Checklist

Create a comprehensive home loan and documentation guide for my client: Client Type: [salaried / self-employed / NRI] Property Type: [under construction / ready to move / resale] Budget Range: ₹[amount] City: [city] Generate: 1. DOCUMENT CHECKLIST: Complete list of documents needed, organized by category (personal, income, property, bank-specific) 2. HOME LOAN COMPARISON: Suggest 5 banks with current approximate interest rates for this profile 3. EMI CALCULATION EXAMPLE: For the given budget at different tenures (15/20/25 years) 4. TAX BENEFITS: Section 80C, 24(b), first-time buyer benefits explained simply 5. COMMON MISTAKES: 5 mistakes Indian home buyers make during loan processing 6. STEP-BY-STEP TIMELINE: From loan application to disbursement — what happens when Write in simple, jargon-free language. The client should be able to follow this independently.
💡 Pro Tip: Share this checklist proactively BEFORE the client asks. It shows expertise and builds trust. Most agents don't help with paperwork guidance — this differentiates you instantly.

🔗 Advanced Workflow Chains

C1

Complete Client Journey Chain (5 Steps)

Run each step in order, feeding output into the next. STEP 1 — Client Profile Builder: "I have a new real estate client with these details: Name: [Name], Budget: ₹[budget], Looking for: [property type], in [area/city], Timeline: [when they want to move], Must-haves: [requirements]. Build a detailed client profile including likely lifestyle, commute needs, family situation, and decision-making style." STEP 2 — Curated Shortlist Presentation: "Based on this client profile, I have these properties to show: [list 3-5 options]. Write a personalized presentation for each property explaining why it matches THIS specific client's needs. Rank them in order of fit." STEP 3 — Site Visit Preparation: "We're scheduling site visits for the top 3 properties. Create a visit preparation guide: what to highlight at each property, anticipated objections from this client, and talking points that align with their priorities." STEP 4 — Follow-Up Strategy: "The client visited all 3 properties. They seemed most interested in [property] but had concerns about [concern]. Write a 7-day follow-up strategy with specific messages for each day." STEP 5 — Closing Preparation: "The client is ready to move forward with [property]. Create: a price negotiation strategy, a documentation checklist, and a closing email that confirms everything and sets next steps."
💡 Pro Tip: This 5-step chain is your entire client management workflow automated. Save each client's output in a Google Doc. After 10 clients, you'll have a pattern library that makes you faster with every new lead.
C2

New Project Launch Marketing Chain (3 Steps)

STEP 1 — Project Analysis: "A new real estate project is launching: [Project name, builder, location, types of units, price range, key amenities, possession timeline]. Analyze this project: identify the ideal buyer profile, compare with 3 competing projects in the area, and list the top 5 selling points I should emphasize." STEP 2 — Marketing Content Suite: "Based on this analysis, create: 3 Instagram Reel scripts highlighting different selling points, 5 WhatsApp forward messages for my client database, 2 LinkedIn posts announcing the project, and 1 email blast for my subscriber list." STEP 3 — Lead Capture Campaign: "Create a Facebook lead generation ad campaign for this project: 3 ad copy variations (each with headline + primary text + CTA), a landing page content outline, and 5 qualifying questions for the lead form. Target audience should be based on the ideal buyer profile from Step 1."
💡 Pro Tip: Be among the first agents to create content about new launches. The first 2 weeks after a project launches are when 60% of the initial bookings happen. Speed is your competitive advantage.

Google Maps Review Response

Property Investment ROI Calculator Pitch

"Create an investment pitch for this property: Location: [area], Price: ₹[price], Expected rental: ₹[monthly rent], Area appreciation rate: [X]% annually. Calculate: rental yield, 5-year appreciation projection, total ROI vs fixed deposit and mutual funds comparison. Present it as a compelling 'why real estate beats FDs' argument."

Handover Ceremony Social Post

Create excitement — make it feel like an event, not just a flat viewing."
